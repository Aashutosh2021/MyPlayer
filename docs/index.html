Sync Play (Multi-Device Synchronized Playback)

Turn your phones, tablets, or family devices into a synchronized surround-sound party setup. Sync Play streams and synchronizes audio playback across multiple Android devices on the same local Wi-Fi network.

  • Zero-Config NSD Auto-Discovery: Automatically broadcasts and discovers host rooms on the local network without complex setup.
  • Direct IP & Port Join: Connect directly to any host IP with automatic port scanning and socket fallback.
  • Universal Source Sync: Seamlessly matches and synchronizes both local MP3/FLAC files and online YouTube Music streams.
  • Unified Playback Control: Host scrubbing, pausing, and track skipping are instantly mirrored to all client devices with sub-millisecond precision.

Dual Bud Mode: Two Songs, One Earphone

Sharing your earbuds with a friend or partner on a commute or workout no longer means compromising on music taste. Dual Bud Mode utilizes low-level stereo channel routing to play two entirely separate songs into the Left and Right earbuds simultaneously.

  • Independent Channel Routing: Left earbud plays Track A while Right earbud plays Track B.
  • Individual Volume Controls: Each listener adjusts their earbud volume independently without affecting the other.
  • Separate Play/Pause Controls: Pause, resume, or seek each track independently.

Everything you need to know about MyPlayer features, privacy, and compatibility.

What is MyPlayer and how is it different from other music apps?+

MyPlayer is a high-performance Android music player built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Unlike ordinary players that only play local files or lock you into a subscription, MyPlayer combines offline local storage with YouTube Music streaming, multi-device synchronized Sync Play, Dual-Bud earbud splitting, synced lyrics from LRCLIB, and 1000×1000 studio HD artwork in a 100% ad-free experience.

How does Sync Play (Multi-Device Synchronized Playback) work?+

Sync Play lets you connect two or more Android devices on the same local Wi-Fi network. One device acts as the Host (Master Clock) and broadcasts a room via Network Service Discovery (NSD) or direct IP. Client devices join the room and play the exact same music in perfect synchronization with shared playback controls.

What is Dual Bud Mode and how do I use it?+

Dual Bud Mode splits your headphone stereo channels into two independent audio pipelines. You can choose Track A for your left earbud and Track B for your right earbud, each with independent volume sliders and track controls. It allows two people sharing a single pair of earphones to enjoy their own music simultaneously.

Can I download online music for offline listening?+

Yes. With one tap on the download button on any online track, MyPlayer downloads the audio stream directly to your device storage. You can view, manage, and play all your downloads without using any mobile data.

How do real-time synced lyrics work?+

MyPlayer automatically queries the LRCLIB open lyrics database. When time-stamped lyrics are found, they scroll automatically in sync with the song. You can also tap any lyric line to jump the song directly to that timestamp, or fall back to plain text lyrics.

Will this website always download the latest version?+

Yes! This website communicates dynamically with the official GitHub Releases API at runtime to fetch the latest published APK asset. You will always get the latest official release directly from GitHub.

Does MyPlayer contain ads or track my activity?+

No. MyPlayer contains zero ads, zero trackers, and no telemetry. It is completely free and open source under the MIT License, respecting Android Scoped Storage to ensure your music library stays entirely private.

What are the minimum system requirements?+

MyPlayer supports Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer, with target SDK 34 (Android 14) and compilation on SDK 35 (Android 15), delivering smooth 120Hz performance on modern devices.
